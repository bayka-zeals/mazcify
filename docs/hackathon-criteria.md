# Hackathon: Video Generation Track (Co-host by PixVerse)

## Overview

Build a web page or app for **Marketing/E-commerce**, **Gaming**, or **Film & Entertainment** that features an AI-generated video (≥30 seconds, made with PixVerse). The video is a content component — like a product demo or trailer — so the app must offer **real functionality beyond playback** (e.g. interaction, purchasing, or comments).

**Tooling requirement:** Use **TRAE** for prompt/script generation, coding, automation, and workflow orchestration.

### Example Ideas
- A marketing or e-commerce page with an AI ad or product video
- A gaming site with an AI trailer or character showcase
- A film & entertainment page with an AI movie clip or scene breakdown

---

## 📝 Evaluation Criteria

| Criterion | Weight | What It Covers |
|---|---|---|
| **Video Generation Quality** | 40% | Mastery of PixVerse; strong aesthetic quality, creativity, and scene relevance |
| **App/Web Completeness & Integration** | 30% | Fully functional product with smooth UX; video embedded naturally in the user journey (not standalone playback) |
| **TRAE Depth & Efficiency Gain** | 30% | Proficient use of TRAE for prompting, coding, automation, and workflow orchestration; measurable efficiency improvement |

---

## Key Constraints & Notes

- Video must be **≥ 30 seconds** and generated with **PixVerse**
- App must provide **real functionality** beyond video playback
- **TRAE** must be used meaningfully across the build process
